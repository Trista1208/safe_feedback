How is this anonymous?

This page is a plain static website. When you press send, only your message, the topic and mood you picked, and today's date are written to a spreadsheet the team can read. The form never asks who you are, and the spreadsheet has no column for IP addresses, emails or accounts. As with any website, the hosting providers (GitHub, Google) technically handle network traffic, but nothing that identifies you is passed on to or stored for the team.
